Discussion

  • Why is a spectrogram a more natural transformer input than the raw waveform at 16 kHz?
  • Whisper versus CLAP: both take spectrograms. Which head do you want for subtitles, and which for a typed search?
  • Name one fusion choice you would defend if audio is often missing at test time.
  • Audio is 64-D and vision is 256-D. What linear map lets you add them, and what map do you need after a concat?
