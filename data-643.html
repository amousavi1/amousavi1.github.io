DATA 443/643: Advanced Concepts in Large Language Models

American University

This is a complementary course hub. Canvas is the official site for this semester. Questions: mousavi@american.edu.

The listed prerequisite is DATA 427/627. DATA 441/641 and DATA 442/642 are not required; overlap with those courses is intentional so this sequence is self-contained. The semester is project-based.

Class meetings

Two-hour class meetings. Canvas is official for submissions and any date changes. A typical meeting: teach the notes (worked examples at the board), play about 10–20 minutes of the listed video, then the discussion prompts. Labs fill remaining studio time or homework.
