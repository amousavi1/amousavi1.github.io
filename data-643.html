DATA 443/643: Advanced Concepts in Large Language Models

American University

Complementary notes, slides, and labs. Canvas is official for due dates and submissions. The listed prerequisite is DATA 427/627. DATA 441/641 and DATA 442/642 are not required; overlap with those courses is intentional so this sequence is self-contained. The semester is project-based. Questions: mousavi@american.edu.

Weekly Materials

Weeks 1–15 notes, labs, slides, and classroom videos are posted. A weekly meeting is two hours: teach the notes (worked examples at the board), play about 10–20 minutes of the listed video, then the discussion prompts. Labs fill remaining studio time or homework. Labs

  • Lab 1: XOR, Autograd, and a Tiny Embedding Space(PDF)

Videos

  • Karpathy: Intro to Large Language Models (0:00–8:00 in class; finish 0:00–20:00 as homework)
  • MIT 6.S191 Lecture 1: perceptron through loss and gradient descent (homework, ~17:20–44:22)
  • 3Blue1Brown: But what is a neural network? (~19 min)
  • 3Blue1Brown: Gradient descent, how neural networks learn (~21 min)
  • 3Blue1Brown: What is backpropagation really doing? (~14 min, after class)
  • StatQuest: Word Embedding and Word2Vec, Clearly Explained (~16 min)

Additional readings

  • Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville: Deep feedforward networks, Ch. 6 opening and §6.1 XOR
  • Nielsen, Neural Networks and Deep Learning, Ch. 1 (gradient descent; stop before long MNIST)
  • Jurafsky & Martin, Speech and Language Processing, Ch. 5 Embeddings (draft of 19 Aug 2026)
  • Mikolov et al. 2013, Efficient estimation of word representations, §§1–3
  • Stanford CS224N: word vectors and neural nets (copy intuitions; skip the skip-gram Jacobian)
  • CS231N notes: neuron, backprop as gates, SGD

Discussion

  • Intrinsic vs. extrinsic evaluation of embeddings: which number belongs in a project report, and which one is for debugging?
  • Name one way embedding geometry can encode a stereotype. What would you measure with the Lab 1 projection?
  • Why can this course skip DATA 641 and 642 and still start from neurons this week?
  • Goodfellow’s two-ReLU XOR vs. Lab 1’s 33-parameter MLP: what did the extra units buy you that the table already showed?
