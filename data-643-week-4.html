Discussion

  • Search a photo gallery with a typed query: why is a coordinated space the default, and what breaks in a joint concat model?
  • A classifier ViT sees all patches at once. Why is a GPT causal mask the wrong extra?
  • InfoNCE treats the rest of the batch as negatives. What goes wrong if every caption is the word photo?
  • Linear probe versus zero-shot: which one needs a labeled C-way training set, and which one needs the text tower?
