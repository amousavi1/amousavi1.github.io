Cumulative through October 28 (weeks 1--9). The new material is
tidyr, mutating and filtering joins, databases, and strings.

Write your answers in an R script. You may use R and RStudio.
You may \textbf{not} use the internet or your notes.
Load the tidyverse, \texttt{nycflights13}, \texttt{DBI},
\texttt{RSQLite}, and \texttt{dbplyr}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Add the airline name from \texttt{airlines} onto \texttt{flights}.
How many flights still have a missing airline name?

\item How many flights have a \texttt{tailnum} that does not appear in
\texttt{planes}? Use a filtering join, not a \texttt{filter()} on a
hand-built list of tail numbers.

\item Use an in-memory SQLite database, as in the databases lecture.
Copy the tibble below into the database, then from that remote table
keep rows with \texttt{score} of at least 8, show the SQL, collect
the result into a local tibble, and disconnect.

\begin{lstlisting}
labs <- tibble(
  name = c("Ada", "Lin", "Grace", "Alan"),
  score = c(9, 7, 8, 6)
)
\end{lstlisting}

\item On the character vector below, use one regular expression to
replace every token that starts with \texttt{AA} or \texttt{DL},
followed by a dash and three digits, with the word \texttt{hit}.
Leave the other strings unchanged.

\begin{lstlisting}
c("AA-101", "UA-22", "DL-303", "B6-1", "aa-404")
\end{lstlisting}
\end{enumerate}
