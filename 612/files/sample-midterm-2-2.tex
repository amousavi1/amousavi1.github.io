\vspace{0.2em}

Practice exam, questions only. Cumulative through November 11
(weeks 1--11). The material since Midterm 1 is joins, strings,
factors, dates, and statistics (a nested model comparison and
numerical summaries).

Put your answers in an R Markdown document and keep the code visible
(\texttt{knitr::opts\_chunk\$set(echo = TRUE)} in the setup chunk).
You may use your notes, the R help files, and the internet.
Do not discuss the exam with anyone else.
Load the tidyverse. \texttt{mpg} comes with \texttt{ggplot2}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Recode a factor} (8 pts).
In \texttt{mpg}, collapse \texttt{drv} so that \texttt{"f"} and
\texttt{"r"} become \texttt{"two"} and \texttt{"4"} stays
\texttt{"four"}.
Drop unused levels. Report how many cars are in each new level.

\item \textbf{Periods and zones} (8 pts).
\texttt{start} is a date-time in \texttt{America/New\_York}.
Build the instant two weeks and three hours later, using a period.
Also report the length of the interval from \texttt{start} to that
later instant in hours.
Show \texttt{start} again in \texttt{UTC}.

\begin{lstlisting}
start <- ymd_hm("2024-11-03 01:15", tz = "America/New_York")
\end{lstlisting}

\item \textbf{Nested models} (10 pts).
Fit two models on \texttt{mpg}: \texttt{hwy} on \texttt{class} alone,
and \texttt{hwy} on \texttt{class} plus \texttt{displ}.
Compare them with \texttt{anova()}.
In one sentence, say whether \texttt{displ} improves the fit after
\texttt{class} is already in the model. Use the p-value from that
comparison.

\item \textbf{Summaries} (8 pts).
For each \texttt{class} in \texttt{mpg}, report the number of cars,
the median \texttt{hwy}, and the interquartile range of \texttt{hwy}.
Sort by the median, highest first.

\item \textbf{Strings, then a date} (6 pts).
The vector below uses a dot between the pieces and a trailing
letter that is not part of the date.
Remove that letter and parse what remains as a date.
Print the dates.

\begin{lstlisting}
c("2024.03.15x", "2023.12.01x", "2022.07.04x")
\end{lstlisting}
\end{enumerate}
